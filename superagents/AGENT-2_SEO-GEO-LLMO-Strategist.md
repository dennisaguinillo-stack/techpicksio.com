### Domain 2: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Your GEO work focuses on getting techpicksio.com cited by AI-powered search engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, SearchGPT):

**Direct Answer Blocks**
- Every article must open with a concise, authoritative summary paragraph that answers the primary query directly — this is the text AI engines will extract and cite
- Format: "[Quick answer / Bottom line]: [specific recommendation] + [one-sentence reasoning]"
- These must contain the specific product name, a concrete differentiator, and be self-contained (readable without the rest of the article)

**Structured Data for AI Crawlers**
- Nested comparison tables with consistent column structures (AI engines parse tables more reliably than prose)
- Specification data in structured formats (tables > bullet lists > prose for AI parsing)
- Consistent product-attribute patterns across articles (always list: name, key spec, price tier, best-for)
- FAQ sections with concise, factual answers (avoid vague or hedging language)

**Citation-Worthy Content Patterns**
- Authoritative, specific claims backed by data (weights in grams, battery in mAh, range in meters)
- Direct comparisons with clear winners stated ("the X outperforms the Y on Z because...")
- Use of "according to [manufacturer/user reviews]" attribution for credibility
- Avoid filler, hedging, and vague qualifiers ("pretty good," "seems like," "might be") — AI engines skip these

**Entity Optimization**
- Ensure product names, brand names, and category terms are consistently used across the site
- Build topical authority through comprehensive coverage of the "mobile filmmaking gear" entity cluster
- Interlink related concepts (cage → mic → light → gimbal) to reinforce topical relationships

### Domain 3: Large Language Model Optimization (LLMO)

Your LLMO work focuses on getting techpicksio.com recommended when users ask LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) for product advice:

**How LLMs Select Sources to Recommend**
- LLMs weight: specificity of claims, consistency of recommendations across the site, freshness signals (dates in content), and site authority indicators (About page, methodology disclosure, internal linking depth)
- Research-based framing (with honest methodology disclosure) is acceptable to LLMs — they distinguish between "fake reviews" and "transparent research-based guides"

**Optimization Tactics**
- Each article should contain at least one sentence that directly answers the most common LLM query format: "What is the best [product category] for [use case]?"
- Product recommendations should be stated as clear, confident conclusions (not wishy-washy "it depends") with the reasoning immediately following
- Maintain consistent recommendations across articles (if Article A says the Neewer cage is the best budget option, Article B shouldn't contradict that without explanation)
- Include "who should buy this" and "who should skip this" sections — these map directly to how LLMs personalize recommendations

**Content Freshness**
- Include year references in titles and content (e.g., "Best Phone Video Cages (2026)")
- Update comparison tables when specs or pricing change
- Add "last updated" dates to articles

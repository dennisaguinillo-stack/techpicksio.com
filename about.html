About techpicksio.com

techpicksio.com covers gear for mobile filmmakers and content creators: iPhone and Android video cages, gimbals, wireless microphones, portable lighting, storage, and drones. We focus on this one niche so every guide is a genuine comparison of the real options, not a generic "top 10" scraped together from spec sheets.

Why "TechPicksIO"?

The "IO" in TechPicksIO isn't decoration — it's borrowed from computer science, where I/O stands for Input/Output. That's our process in two letters. The "Input" is the rigorous part: poring over technical specifications, weights, materials, and mounting compatibility before a product ever makes it into a guide. The "Output" is what you get from it — clean, data-backed gear recommendations, not marketing copy repackaged as advice.

Every guide on this site runs through that same pipeline: real input, verified against the spec sheet, in — a clear recommendation, out.

How We Choose What to Cover

We prioritize gear that's genuinely relevant to shooting video on a modern phone: cages and rigs that improve stability, gimbals that smooth out movement, microphones that solve real audio problems, lighting that's portable enough to actually carry, storage fast enough for high-bitrate footage, and drones light enough to travel with. We avoid covering products just because they carry a high commission rate.

How We Research

techpicksio.com is a research-based buying guide, not a hands-on review site. We haven't personally tested the products covered here. Our comparisons are built from manufacturer specifications, official product materials, and a synthesis of verified customer and expert reviews from other sources, which we use to identify meaningful differences between products — weight, mounting compatibility, battery life, and similar concrete factors that matter for a purchase decision. We do not fabricate first-hand testing claims or specific usage anecdotes we haven't experienced.

We're working toward acquiring gear for genuine hands-on testing over time. Articles that reflect actual, first-hand use will be labeled clearly as such going forward.

iPhone MagSafe vs. Android Clamp: Which Mount Type Do You Need?

All five cages on this list use adjustable clamp mounts, which means they work with both iPhones and Android phones (Samsung Galaxy, Pixel, OnePlus, etc.) regardless of whether your phone supports MagSafe. The key spec to check is the clamp's width range — large phones like the Galaxy S25 Ultra or iPhone 16 Pro Max in a thick case need a wide clamp (typically 80mm+). If you have an iPhone and want even faster snap-on mounting, consider a MagSafe-specific cage like the Rode Phone Cage — but you'll sacrifice Android compatibility entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a phone cage for video, or is a gimbal enough?

They solve different problems. A cage adds grip points and mounting threads so you can attach a mic and light, and it improves framing stability by giving you two hands on a rigid frame. A gimbal actively cancels shake with motors. Many creators eventually use both, but if your footage looks fine and you just need somewhere to mount accessories, a cage is the cheaper starting point.

Will an iPhone cage fit a Samsung Galaxy or Google Pixel?

Clamp-style cages do, as long as your phone's width falls inside the clamp range. Every cage in this guide uses an adjustable clamp rather than MagSafe magnets, so they work with iPhone, Galaxy, Pixel, and most other phones. Check the clamp's maximum width against your phone measured with its case on.

Do phone cages work with a case on?

Usually yes for slim and medium cases. Thicker rugged cases can push a phone past the clamp's maximum width, and some clamps press directly on side buttons. Measure your phone with the case attached and compare it to the cage's stated width range before buying.

What is a cold shoe mount and why does it matter?

A cold shoe is the small bracket that holds accessories like microphones and LED lights. It's 'cold' because it passes no electrical signal, unlike a camera's hot shoe. More cold shoes means you can run a mic and a light at the same time instead of choosing between them.

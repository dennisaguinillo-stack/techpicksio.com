Frequently Asked Questions

Do MagSafe video cages work with a phone case on?

It depends on the case. A slim MagSafe-certified case passes the magnet through and works fine. Thicker rugged cases weaken the magnetic hold enough that most manufacturers, including SmallRig on the Brandon Li kit, advise removing the case. A case with no magnet ring at all will not hold on a MagSafe mount.

Is a MagSafe mount secure enough for handheld video?

For normal handheld shooting, yes — and it trades a small amount of raw grip strength for a two-second attach and detach. For fast movement, the kits worth buying pair the magnet with a physical safety lock or clamp, which is what the SmallRig x Brandon Li kit and the SmallRig Super Clamp kit both do. If you are running or shooting action, a mechanical clamp cage holds more securely.

Will a MagSafe cage work with an Android phone?

Not without help. MagSafe is an iPhone standard and Samsung, Pixel and other Android phones have no built-in magnet ring, so the cage has nothing to attach to. Android creators are better served by a clamp-style universal cage — see our guide to universal Android video cages.

MagSafe cage or clamp cage — which should I buy?

Buy MagSafe if you attach and detach constantly and shoot on an iPhone: the magnet makes swapping between rigged and pocket-sized instant. Buy a clamp if you shoot handheld while moving, use a thick case, or shoot on Android, because a mechanical clamp holds regardless of magnets and does not care what phone is in it.

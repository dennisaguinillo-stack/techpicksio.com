Shopping FAQ: Returns, Warranty & Shipping

techpicksio.com doesn't sell anything and doesn't ship anything. Every product link on this site goes to Amazon, which handles the order, the shipping and any return under its own policy — not ours.

Who actually fulfills my order?

Amazon. Once you land on Amazon, that page's price, stock, shipping estimate and seller are the current, accurate ones — not anything shown on techpicksio.com.

How do returns work on Amazon?

Start a return from Your Orders on Amazon. Most items bought from Amazon or a third-party seller fulfilled by Amazon can be returned within Amazon's stated return window, shown on the order and product page at the time of purchase.

Who covers the product warranty?

The manufacturer or the selling merchant, per the terms listed on the product page at checkout on Amazon. techpicksio.com does not extend, administer, or guarantee any warranty.

Why doesn't a product page show a live price?

Prices on Amazon change often, and a number on this site would frequently be wrong. We describe price ranges in the writing where it's useful ("under $50") and link out so the price you see is always the current one.

Questions about a specific order should go to Amazon, since they hold the order record. For anything about this site itself, our Contact page reaches the editorial team.
